What does a vape store POS do?

A point of sale (POS) system is an upgrade from the traditional cash register. It records all transactions and processes all types of payments. In addition, a vape store POS keeps track of inventory in real-time, integrates with third-party applications and manages daily operations. 

What is the best vape store POS system?

Vape stores need a point of sale system that not only processes payments but also makes running the business easier. The best POS system for a vape store will bring security, simplicity, and speed.

The system should also run on the cloud. With cloud access, you can access your POS from anywhere since all data is stored on secure remote servers.

With POS integrations, you can make running your vape store easier. Marketing programs like Mailchimp allow you to interact with customers and stay top of mind and accounting integrations like QuickBooks make it easy to export all financial data. Lastly, loyalty schemes help you reward and retain customers.

How does inventory management work on Epos Now POS?

Epos Now software updates as you trade in real-time through the cloud, so you always know exactly how much of your favourite products you have in stock.

Once you set up an initial stock level, you can set a minimum restock level and receive email alerts every morning so you know which products to stock up on.

You can track stock in numbered, liquid, and weighed measures and can use the same stock to sell multiple products for complex inventory requirements that work across any industry.

Finally, you can update your stock levels from the sales screen, your back office, and through purchase orders so that updating your stock takes moments when you receive an order.
