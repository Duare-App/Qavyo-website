Pricing questions

How does regional pricing work?

Qavyo assigns a pricing region to your business from country and commercial context. Prices shown here are for our standard region; the checkout amount is shown in your currency before you subscribe.

What is included in the free trial?

Every plan capability and add-on is available for 30 days. No card is required to start.

Can I add features later?

Yes. Add-ons such as Loyalty, Coupons, Payroll, Qavyo AI Intelligent and Mobile App can be enabled when you need them — see the full add-on list for what each one includes.

Do you limit staff users?

No. Qavyo does not charge by staff seat. Add the people your business needs.

What's the difference between Monthly and Lifetime?

Monthly is billed every month, cancel anytime. Lifetime is one payment, once, for permanent access — support is included either way, for as long as you're using Qavyo.
