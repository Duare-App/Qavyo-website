Pricing questions

How does regional pricing work?

Qavyo assigns a pricing region to your business from country and commercial context. Prices shown here are for Tier 3; Tier 2 and Tier 1 amounts are shown on each plan and confirmed before payment.

What is included in the free trial?

Every plan capability is available for 30 days. No card is required to start.

Can I add features later?

Yes, by moving up a plan. There are no add-ons: Growth adds loyalty, workforce tools and full Intelligence; Business adds the apps, SMS, a custom domain and unlimited branches. See what's in each plan for the full list.

Do you limit staff users?

No. Add as many staff as you need on every plan, and every staff member can use the Staff Portal. Scheduling, clock-in and payroll come with Growth; the Staff App comes with Business.

What's the difference between Monthly and Lifetime?

Monthly is billed every month, cancel anytime. Lifetime is one payment, once, for permanent access — support is included either way, for as long as you're using Qavyo.
